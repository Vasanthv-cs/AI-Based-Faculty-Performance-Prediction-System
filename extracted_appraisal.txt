The requirements for the each scale of evaluation for various cadres viz. assistant professor, associate professor and professor varies in accordance with the scope of work of the respective cadre.

An assistant professor can score 10 points if a research paper is communicated to a SCI/WOS/Scopus indexed /UGC care list journals, whereas the faculty members of other cadres shall score 10 points only on publishing one paper in SCI/WOS/Scopus indexed /UGC care list journals.

Assistant and associate professors shall score 15 points if a publication is made in SCI/WOS/Scopus indexed /UGC care list journals.

The requirement of a professor is 2 publications during the assessment period to score 15 points.

The publications claimed shall be considered only if the faculty claiming is either a first or a corresponding author.

The publications made in collaboration with academics in foreign universities/ Industries, does not require a faculty claiming to be a first or corresponding author.
